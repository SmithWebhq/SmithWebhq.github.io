SmithWebHQ, a sole proprietor web services business, uses text messaging to answer customers who contact us. If you call our business number and we miss your call, our system sends you one text so you can reply with what you need. Your replies reach the owner, who answers personally.

You are only messaged if you contacted us first, by calling or texting our business number. Agreeing to receive texts is not a condition of buying anything.

3. Message frequency

Frequency varies by conversation. Typically one text-back after a missed call, plus ordinary back-and-forth replies.

Message and data rates may apply. These are charged by your mobile carrier, not by us.

5. Opting out

Reply STOP to cancel at any time. After you send STOP, we may send one final message confirming you are opted out, and then nothing more. Reply HELP for help, or email Smithwebhq@gmail.com.

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Texting may not work on landlines or some devices.

7. Privacy

How we handle your number is covered in our SMS Privacy Policy. The short version: we use it only to reply to you, and no mobile information is shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
