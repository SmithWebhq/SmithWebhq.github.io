SMS Privacy Policy

SmithWebHQ · Effective August 17, 2026

This policy covers text messages (SMS) sent and received through the SmithWebHQ business phone number.

What we collect

Only the phone number you use when you call or text us, and the content of the messages you choose to send us. We do not collect phone numbers from websites, forms, purchased lists, or any other source.

How we use it

Your number is used for one thing: replying to you. If you call us and we miss your call, we send you one text so you can tell us what you need. After that, we text only as part of our conversation with you.

What we never do

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. We do not sell, rent, or trade your phone number or message content. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third parties.

Message frequency and rates

Message frequency varies by conversation — typically one text-back after a missed call, plus replies while we sort out what you need. Message and data rates may apply, charged by your mobile carrier.

Your choices

Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages from us. Reply HELP for help. You can also just tell us to stop texting you, and we will.

Contact

Questions about this policy: email Smithwebhq@gmail.com.
